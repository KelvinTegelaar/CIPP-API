Insurers ask whether administrators use a separate account for admin work. This check lists enabled accounts that hold a directory role and also have a mailbox or a licence, which usually means the same account is used for email and daily work.

**Remediation Action**

1. Give each administrator a separate, unlicensed admin account with no mailbox.
2. Remove directory roles from the daily-use account, or make them eligible through Privileged Identity Management or JIT Admin.
